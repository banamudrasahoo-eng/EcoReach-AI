⚙️ EcoReach AI 7-Step Processing Pipeline

1

User Input Ingestion

User submits a query regarding waste, energy, water, or general campus eco-practices.

2

Query Understanding & Intent Parsing

Pre-processing, token normalization, and entity extraction to parse action verbs and target items.

3

Sustainability Domain Classification

Categorizes input into Waste Management, Energy Conservation, Water Sustainability, or General Awareness.

4

Knowledge Processing & Policy Mapping

Matches domain criteria against SDG 12/11/13 guidelines and institutional recycling standards.

5

Recommendation Generation

Synthesizes actionable steps, single-point sustainable habits, and estimated impacts.

6

Responsible AI Safety & Guardrail Check

Filters out unsafe advice, detects domain relevance, and flags unverified items for manual checking.

7

Final Sustainability Recommendation

Delivers formatted structured response with Category, Recommendation, Action Items, and Sustainable Tip.

📝 AI Prompt Workflow Demonstration

Below is the exact structured prompt architecture utilized for Large Language Model (LLM) orchestration (such as IBM Granite).

[SYSTEM ROLE]
"You are EcoReach AI, a campus sustainability assistant built for educational institutions aligned with SDG 12, SDG 11, and SDG 13."

[USER INPUT]
"How can I reduce electricity consumption in my classroom?"

[EXECUTION PROCESS]
1. Identify domain: Energy Conservation
2. Extract relevant variables: Classroom setting, lighting, fan, AC usage
3. Generate practical actions: Daylight harvesting, AC thermostat optimization
4. Responsible AI check: Ensure no misleading efficiency percentage claims
5. Formulate final response structure: Category, AI Recommendation, Actions, Sustainable Action

[STRUCTURED OUTPUT GENERATED]
Category: Energy Conservation
AI Recommendation: "Reducing unnecessary electricity use supports sustainable campus operations."
Recommended Actions:
1. Switch off unused lights and fans when exiting.
2. Make better use of natural daylight during daytime classes.
3. Maintain AC temperature at an eco-friendly 24°C.
4. Monitor daily classroom power usage.
Sustainable Action: "Start a classroom energy-saving checklist."
